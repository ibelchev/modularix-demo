§ 1

Who we are

Modularix is responsible for your personal data as its controller. A15

Modularix is a Synotech company. You can reach us about anything in this policy at office@modularix.com or on +40 722 598 251. Our full company details are in the legal notice.

Content needed: registered name and office of the legal entity, and a data protection officer contact if one is appointed.
§ 2

What we collect

We collect only what you give us, and what any web server records when a page is requested:

  • Quote requests: your name, work email, company, the solution you are interested in and any details you add.
  • Correspondence: what you send us by email, and notes of phone calls about your project.
  • Server logs: your IP address, browser, the page requested and the time, kept by our hosting provider to run and secure the site.

This website has no analytics, advertising trackers or social media plugins, and sets no cookies. Fonts, scripts and images are served from our own domain, so visiting it sends nothing to third parties. See the cookie policy.

Please don't send us sensitive data (such as health data) through the form or by email; we don't need it to quote for a data centre.

§ 3

Why we use it

Each use has a legal basis under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quote: steps you ask us to take before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep a record of our business correspondence and follow up on it: our legitimate interest in running our business (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the website secure and working: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet accounting, tax and other legal duties if you become a customer: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We don't sell personal data, we don't use it for automated decisions or profiling, and we don't send marketing emails unless you have asked for them.

§ 4

Who sees it

Your data is seen by the Modularix team handling your enquiry and, where your project needs them, by engineers at other Synotech group companies. We also use service providers that process data on our behalf under a contract (Art. 28 GDPR), such as our website host and email provider. We disclose data to public authorities only where the law requires it.

§ 5

Where it is kept

We aim to keep your data within the European Economic Area. Where a provider transfers it outside the EEA, we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission or on the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Content needed: the hosting and email providers and where they store data.
§ 6

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a contract: up to three years after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Customer correspondence and contract records: for the life of the contract, then for as long as Romanian law requires us to keep them.
  • Server logs: for a short period set by the hosting provider, for security only.
Content needed: confirmed retention periods, including the server log period.
§ 7

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you or another organisation in a portable format. You can also object to uses based on our legitimate interest (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR).

Write to office@modularix.com. We reply within one month, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

§ 8

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
